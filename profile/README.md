# Couplet

**Simulation and reality, in step.** Couplet is a vendor-neutral home for open physical-AI tools: AI-driven PCB and mechanical design, hardware-in-the-loop testing, robot training in simulation, and the bridges that keep a digital twin and the real thing in agreement.

Projects here were created at [Exowatt](https://exowatt.com) and are governed in the open — contributions, co-maintainers and partner organizations are welcome. See each repository for its status, prerequisites (proprietary SDKs such as SOLIDWORKS or NVIDIA Isaac Sim are never bundled) and license.

For Exowatt's developer and agent tooling, see [exowatt-oss](https://github.com/exowatt-oss).
